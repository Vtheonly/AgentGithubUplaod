**A1.3 — Real parser defects found while exonerating the engine [STATIC] (BUG-052):**
`positive-number.ts:39-43`:
- `s.replace(/[DA€$]/gi, "")` is a **character class** — it deletes the letters `D` and `A` ANYWHERE in a string, not just a trailing "DA" suffix. Any text cell containing stray D/A letters silently loses them before `Number()` (a "1D500" style typo becomes 1500 instead of an honest error).
- The FR **thousands dot is not handled**: a text cell `"1.500"` (French formatting of 1 500) parses as **1.5** — a ÷1000 corruption on the text path (numeric cells are unaffected; the S8 oracle ran numeric cells). `"1.500,00"` → `"1.500.00"` → NaN (honest error).
- Fix path: treat `.` as thousands separator only when followed by exactly 3 digits and another separator/end; restrict currency-symbol stripping to trailing `DA`/`DZD` tokens.

**A1.4 — The ×10 "display illusion" candidate [STATIC]:** `formatDzd(amount, {compact:true})` renders `125 000 DZD` as **"125 k DZD"** (`currency.ts:14-18`) on 10+ dashboard surfaces (S11-C census: `debt-aging-tab.tsx:327,487`, `financials-page.tsx:392-422`, `cash-flow-radar.tsx:59,79`, `payroll-funding-card.tsx:106,117`, `insights-rail.tsx:184,187`, `debtors-pareto-card.tsx:82-83`, `pivot-matrix-card.tsx:205`). A user reading "125 k" beside an Excel "125 000" can perceive digit corruption. The remediation plan: keep compact only on KPI tiles, never beside raw workbook comparisons.
